TechGadget Inc. — Internal Company Policy Document
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Classification: Internal Use Only

SECTION 1: CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------------------
All TechGadget employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional
conduct in all business interactions. This includes treating colleagues, customers,
and partners with respect and fairness.

Employees must not engage in discriminatory behavior, harassment of any kind,
or conduct that could damage the company's reputation or relationships.

SECTION 2: DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY
------------------------------------
2.1 Customer Data
Employees who have access to customer personal data are responsible for protecting
it in accordance with applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA) and our internal data
handling procedures.

Customer data must not be shared externally without written authorization from
the Privacy Officer and must be accessed only for legitimate business purposes.

2.2 Confidential Business Information
Business strategies, financial data, product roadmaps, and personnel information
are strictly confidential. Employees must not disclose such information to any
external parties, including family members.

2.3 Incident Reporting
Any suspected data breach, security incident, or unauthorized access must be
reported to security@techgadget.example.com within 2 hours of discovery.

SECTION 3: ACCEPTABLE USE OF TECHNOLOGY
-----------------------------------------
3.1 Company Devices
Company-issued devices are for business use. Personal use is permitted in moderation
but must not interfere with work responsibilities or violate any policies.

3.2 Software Installation
Installing unauthorized software on company devices is prohibited. Contact IT
at helpdesk@techgadget.example.com to request software installation approval.

3.3 AI Tools & Chatbots
Employees using AI-powered tools must not input confidential company data,
customer personal data, or trade secrets into external AI services.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY
-------------------------------
Employees approved for remote work must ensure their home workspace is secure:
- Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
- Use a secure, private WiFi network (not public hotspots)
- Ensure no unauthorized individuals can view confidential information

SECTION 5: CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
----------------------------------
Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest to their manager and HR.
This includes investments in competitor companies, outside employment in related
industries, or personal relationships with vendors or customers.

SECTION 6: POLICY VIOLATIONS
------------------------------
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination of employment, depending on the nature and severity of the violation.
Legal action may be pursued for violations involving theft of trade secrets or
customer data.

For questions about this policy, contact hr@techgadget.example.com.
