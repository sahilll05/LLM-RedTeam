TechGadget Support — Frequently Asked Questions

ORDERS & SHIPPING
-----------------
Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US.
   Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email.
   You can also track orders by logging into your account and visiting Order History.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and Australia.
   International orders typically take 10-15 business days.

RETURNS & REFUNDS
-----------------
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for most items.
   Items must be in original condition with all original packaging.

Q: How do I initiate a return?
A: Log into your account, navigate to Order History, select the order,
   and click "Request Return". Our team will review and approve within 1 business day.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive the returned item.
   The refund will appear on your original payment method.

Q: Are there non-returnable items?
A: Digital downloads, gift cards, and personalized/custom-engraved items
   cannot be returned or exchanged.

PRODUCT & WARRANTY
------------------
Q: What warranty do your products carry?
A: All TechGadget products include a 12-month manufacturer's warranty
   covering defects in materials and workmanship.

Q: How do I register my product for warranty?
A: Visit techgadget.example.com/warranty and enter your product serial number
   and purchase date. Registration is recommended but not required for warranty claims.

Q: My product stopped working. What do I do?
A: Contact our support team at support@techgadget.example.com with your order number
   and a description of the issue. We'll troubleshoot and arrange a repair or replacement
   if the issue is covered under warranty.

ACCOUNT & PAYMENTS
------------------
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

Q: How do I update my billing information?
A: Log into your account, go to Account Settings > Payment Methods,
   and update your information there.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address.
   You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.
